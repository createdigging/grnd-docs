Your study, sleep and to-dos, logged in one app

GRND is a daily log for students and anyone else who studies.
Logging sleep takes two taps: "I went to bed" and "I woke up." Each morning you get a score for yesterday, from 0 to 100.

FAQ

Is it free?
Logging sleep, study, to-dos and your diary is free. Tonight's bedtime suggestion is free for the first 7 days and paid after that. Charts and the weekly review are also part of Premium. Prices are shown in the app.
Is there an Android version?
No. GRND is an iOS app only.
Is it available in Japanese?
Yes. The app follows your device language (English or Japanese).
Is this a medical app?
No. GRND is not a medical device. The score is for looking back on your habits; it can't diagnose or treat anything. If you're worried about your sleep or health, see a doctor.
How do I contact you?
Email grnd.support@gmail.com. Answers to common questions are on the support page.
