Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 August 2026 / Last updated: 20 August 2026

Note on language. The Japanese version of this Policy is authoritative. This English translation is provided for convenience; if the two differ, the Japanese text governs.

GRND (the "App") is an app for steadying your daily rhythm by recording sleep and study. This Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is handled.

In short

  • What you record is stored on your device, and synced to the cloud only when you are signed in.
  • No other user can see your data.
  • Usage is measured only in a form that does not identify you.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from inside the App.
  • No location data is collected. There are no ads, and your data is never passed to third parties for advertising.

1. Operator

The App is run by an individual developer. For enquiries about this Policy, use the contact below. For requests such as disclosure of retained personal data, we will verify your identity and respond without undue delay, including providing the operator's name.

Contact: grnd.support@gmail.com

3. Purposes of use

  1. To provide the App's features — storing, displaying, and aggregating records, and calculating scores and charts
  2. To sync data across multiple devices (when signed in)
  3. To deliver the notifications you set (bedtime, to-do due dates, and so on)
  4. To understand statistics such as retention and improve the App (anonymous usage only)
  5. To respond to enquiries

Information collected is not used beyond the purposes above.

4. Where data is stored, and how it moves

What you record is first stored on your device, in the app's own storage area. When you are signed in, it is additionally synced to a cloud database (Supabase).

On the cloud side, access is restricted per account (row level security), so no one can reach data belonging to another account.

Using the App requires an account. Records are stored on your device and synced to the cloud.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Except where required by law, your personal information is not disclosed to third parties. Your data is never sold or rented, and never used for advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is retained while your account exists. You can delete it at any time as follows.

  • Delete your account and all data — from the "Profile" screen in the App. This deletes your cloud account and every record linked to it. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete only the data on your device — delete the app from your device.
  • After deletion, data cannot be restored from a backup. If you need a copy of your records, contact grnd.support@gmail.com before deleting.

Because anonymous usage records cannot identify an individual, they may remain as statistics after account deletion.

8. Security

  • All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data in the cloud is protected by per-account access restrictions.
  • Data on your device is stored in the per-app protected area provided by the OS.

That said, complete security cannot be guaranteed for communication over the internet or for electronic storage.

9. Your rights

You may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, or suspension of use of your personal information. What can be done inside the App (editing data, deleting your account) you can do yourself. For anything else, contact grnd.support@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond in accordance with the law.

10. Age

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please do not use it. Minors should use it with the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 13, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be revised as laws change or features are added. Significant changes will be announced on this page. Revised content applies from the time it is posted here.
